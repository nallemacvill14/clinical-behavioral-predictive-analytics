==============================================================================
POWER BI DAX MEASURES: CLINICAL BEHAVIORAL OPERATIONS DASHBOARD
==============================================================================

1. Active Enrolled Patients:
Total Cohort Size = COUNTROWS(clinical_cohort)

2. Clinical Success Rate (%):
Treatment Success Rate = 
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(clinical_cohort), clinical_cohort[treatment_response] = 1),
    COUNTROWS(clinical_cohort),
    0
)

3. Mean Adherence Percentage:
Average Adherence Rate = AVERAGE(clinical_cohort[adherence_rate_pct])

4. High Impulsivity / Attentional Bias Alert Count:
High Attentional Risk Count = 
CALCULATE(
    COUNTROWS(clinical_cohort),
    clinical_cohort[attentional_bias_score] >= 0.80
)

5. Mean PsychoPy Reaction Time (ms):
Average Cognitive RT = AVERAGE(clinical_cohort[psychopy_mean_rt_ms])
